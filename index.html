About Pokkisham Oil Mill

Pokkisham Cold Pressed Oil Mill & Grocery Store was founded in Trichy, Tamil Nadu with a clear vision: to bring back authentic, unrefined, traditional wooden cold pressed oils (Chekku Ennai) and chemical-free organic farm produce to modern households.

Our oils are extracted slowly using traditional Vagai wood chekku without heating, refining, or bleaching. This preserves vital nutrients, natural aroma, and rich antioxidants essential for heart health and natural longevity.

Our Guarantee: 100% Pure, Zero Chemicals, Zero Adulteration, Direct Farm-to-Home Freshness.
